The Practice

Craft first, AI as the accelerant.

Terico Harper has spent thirteen years building a production practice the traditional way: the shot lists, the on-site shoots, the late-night edits, for municipalities, tourism organizations, and brands.

AI didn't replace any of that. It removed the bottleneck. Ideation, scripting, and editing that used to eat a week now move in an afternoon, so the creative energy goes back into the story, not the software.

His work sits at the intersection of civic documentation and applied AI. Generative tools accelerate the process, but the pipeline still runs on real access: City Council members, small-business owners, and community partners who trust him to show up and get it right.
